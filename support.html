Notifications & briefings

I'm not getting notifications
  1. Make sure you're signed in to Ground Truth.
  2. Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Notifications → Ground Truth.
  3. Check that you follow at least one source in Profile → Source Selection. Alerts are only sent for new stories from sources you follow.
How do daily and weekly briefings work?

Turn them on in Profile → Briefings. The daily briefing arrives around 8am your local time with the top stories from your sources. The weekly briefing arrives Monday mornings and includes Numbers of the week. If you turn on both, Monday's weekly briefing takes the place of that day's daily one, so you only get one notification each morning.

How do I add the Home Screen widget?

Long-press an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Ground Truth, and choose a size. You can also long-press the Ground Truth app icon and pick a widget size. The widget shows today's briefing for the sources you follow.

Sources & summaries

How do I change my sources?

Go to Profile → Source Selection. Sources are listed A to Z, and you can search by name. Your feed, briefings, widget, and alerts all follow your selection.

What does "See it in the source" do?

Each key fact is checked against the original article word for word. Tapping "See it in the source" opens the article in Safari at the exact sentence the fact came from.

I think a summary or fact is wrong

Please let us know at support@groundtruthai.app with the article title or a screenshot. Accuracy is the whole point of Ground Truth, so we review every report.

Newsletter

I signed up but didn't get the confirmation email

Check your Promotions and Spam folders for an email from briefing@mail.groundtruthai.app, then click "Confirm subscription". You can sign up again on the homepage to resend it.

How do I unsubscribe?

Use the Unsubscribe link at the bottom of any Ground Truth Weekly email, or turn off "Weekly email newsletter" in the app under Profile → Briefings.

Account

Where are my saved articles?

Go to Profile → Saved Articles. Save an article with the bookmark button on the article page, or by swiping left on a story in the feed. Saved articles sync to your account.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, settings, saved articles, newsletter subscription, and notification history. If iOS asks you to sign in again first, sign out, sign back in, and try again. See the Privacy Policy for details.
