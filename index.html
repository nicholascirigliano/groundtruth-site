FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where does Ground Truth get its news?

Directly from official company sources, such as the blogs and newsrooms of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Meta, AWS, Apple, Mistral AI, Hugging Face and others. You choose which ones you follow.

How are the summaries written?

An AI model summarizes the full text of each announcement, using only what the article says. Summaries are neutral by design: no hype, opinions or speculation.

What does "verified key facts" mean?

Each key fact comes with a quote from the article. Before it's shown, Ground Truth checks that the quote appears in the original word for word, and drops any fact that can't be matched. Tap "See it in the source" to read the exact sentence.

Can I get the news without the app?

Yes. Ground Truth Weekly is a free email every Monday with the week's top stories and its most important numbers.
