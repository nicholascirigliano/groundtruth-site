Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

The short version
  • We collect only what's needed to run Ground Truth: your account, your settings, and basic usage analytics.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • You can delete your account and server-side data from inside the app at any time.

This policy explains how Ground Truth ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Ground Truth iOS app, the website at groundtruthai.app, and the Ground Truth Weekly email newsletter.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a user ID and the email address associated with your sign-in (with Sign in with Apple, this may be a private relay address).

Settings and saved items

  • The news sources you choose to follow.
  • Your daily and weekly briefing preferences, and your device's time zone (so briefings arrive around 8am your time).
  • Articles you save.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your device's push notification token and a record of which notifications and briefings were sent to it.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to Ground Truth Weekly, we store your email address, your subscription status, where you signed up (website or app), and when you confirmed or unsubscribed.

Usage analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used, for example when a briefing or article is opened, an article is saved, or a setting is changed. Firebase also collects standard information such as app version, device model, operating system, approximate region, and an app-specific identifier. We do not collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

Stored only on your device

Your reading streak, the time of your last visit (used for the "You're all caught up" marker), and a copy of your selected sources used by the Home Screen widget are kept on your device and are not sent to us.

2. How we use information

  • To provide your personalized feed, briefings, widget, and saved articles.
  • To send the push notifications and briefings you turn on.
  • To send Ground Truth Weekly if you subscribe, including a one-time confirmation email.
  • To understand which features are used so we can improve the app, and to keep the service secure and reliable.

We do not sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

3. Service providers

We rely on a small number of providers to run Ground Truth. They process data on our behalf and only for these purposes.

ProviderWhat it's used for
Google FirebaseSign-in (Authentication), settings and saved articles (Firestore), push delivery (Cloud Messaging, with Apple Push Notification service), and usage analytics (Analytics)
RailwayHosting for our backend and database (notification tokens and history, newsletter subscribers, articles and summaries)
ResendSending newsletter and confirmation emails (receives your email address and the email content)
OpenAISummarizing publicly available articles. Only article text is sent; no information about you is shared.
GitHub PagesHosting this website

4. Data retention

  • Account, settings, and saved articles: kept until you delete your account.
  • Notification tokens and history: kept while your account is active, and deleted when you delete your account.
  • Newsletter: kept while you're subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we keep your email address and unsubscribe status so we don't email you again.
  • Articles and summaries (public content, not personal data): kept for about 180 days.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Change your sources and briefings anytime in the app.
  • Turn off notifications in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter with the link in any email, or with the toggle in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete Account. This deletes your sign-in account, your settings and saved articles, your newsletter subscription, your registered devices, and your notification history.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your data by emailing us at the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; contact us and we'll help.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Viewing or changing your newsletter subscription from the app, and deleting your account data, require your verified sign-in.

7. Children

Ground Truth is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above, and where appropriate let you know in the app.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: privacy@groundtruthai.app
